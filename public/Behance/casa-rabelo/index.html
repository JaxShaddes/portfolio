Casa Rabelo

A Brazilian wine boutique imbued with the essential soul of Portugal.

Project Overview

The brand is anchored in the heritage of the Douro River and the iconic Rabelo vessels that navigated its currents, transporting the region's essence. This duality celebrates the enduring link between history, meticulous craft, and the act of discovery.

Distilled Silhouette / Visual Emblem

A rigorously minimalist emblem derived from the silhouette of the Rabelo craft, anchored by precise geometric typography to signify both measured intent and modern luxury.
